| Action | Domains |

The full breakdown with domains, company names and headcount is in
`apollo_domain_actions.csv`.

## For phone numbers specifically

Apollo's **company** phone adds nothing. Against the number already on our
Google Maps row:

| | Domains |
|---|---|
| Identical to what we already have | 48 |
| Different | 31 |
| A number Apollo has and we do not | **0** |

Our list already carries a phone on 223 of 227 rows, straight from Maps,
at no credit cost. Several of the 31 "different" are the parent-company
rows, where Apollo's number is a head office in another state -- worse
than what we have, not better.

**What Apollo gives that Maps cannot is the direct dial**: a named
person's direct line or mobile, rather than the front desk. 312 direct
dial credits have already been consumed this cycle, so that route is
already in use. That is the only phone worth spending the remaining
credits on.

## How to spend the 236 lead credits left

They reset 3 Dec. Spend them on direct dials at the **63 KEEP domains**
only, largest first -- headcount is in the CSV, from Sunpro at 750 down to
single-operator shops.

**Do not spend credits on the 15 CAUTION domains.** A direct dial there
reaches TruTeam in Florida, Glass Doctor in Texas, Synergy in Atlanta or
Window Depot in Ohio. For those, the Maps number we already hold is the
local branch and is the better number.

## Two more catches

`clearview-window.com` is in our list as **Clearview Windows and Doors**,
but Apollo holds it as **Clearview Window *Cleaning***. One of the two is
wrong about what this business does, and window cleaning would not belong
in the list. Worth a look before dialling.

`utahwindowcompany.com` ("Salt Lake Window Company LLC", Salt Lake City)
is a real Utah window company that Apollo found, but it is not on our
list -- our similarly named row is **Salt Lake City Window Company** on
`saltlakewindow.com`. Either Apollo matched the wrong business, or this is
a genuine company the scrape missed.
